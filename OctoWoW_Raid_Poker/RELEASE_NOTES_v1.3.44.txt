OctoWoW Raid Poker v1.3.44
New: configurable Ante in Poker Settings (0-999999 virtual gold).
Default: 10g. Raid Leader changes value by typing and pressing Enter.
Each eligible player including Dealer pays Ante before dealing.
Players unable to afford Ante are excluded from the hand.
Ante contributes to pot/side pots, not to first-round street bet.
Ready Check remains removed. All 52 Classic Poker cards retained.
Not yet verified in the live OctoWoW client.
